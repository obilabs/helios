# ObiLabs phishing drill + detection vs KnowBe4 — positioning brief (2026-09-04)

**Evidence base.** Three briefs: *complaints* (~45 Reddit threads, full comment trees via Arctic Shift/pullpush + Trustpilot/Capterra/G2/HN), *likes* (41 threads, ~3,500 comments, 2020-Aug 2026, full trees), *switching* (Reddit **titles/snippets only** + HN/Tildes/Slashdot/Google Admin Community/GoPhish issues/KnowBe4 KB/UCSD + ETH Zurich studies). Weight: the two full-read briefs over the snippet brief where they conflict. Reddit "alternatives" threads since 2025 are heavily astroturfed; named admins with specifics were weighted over one-liners. Thread refs are `r/<sub>/<id>` → `reddit.com/comments/<id>`.

**Reconciled discrepancies.**
- *Pushy sales*: the snippet brief found no evidence and said don't lead with it; both full-read briefs found it in ~14 threads / 19 comments, top-voted (r/sysadmin/vd4b6o, wlnb0e, o18z4q; r/msp/intcld, 10m1f6y). Verdict: **real and loud**, but it is a commercial-posture gap, not a product gap.
- *Gmail-API injection as differentiator*: the complaints brief says "say so"; the switching brief shows KnowBe4 already ships Direct Message Injection for Google (DWD + `gmail.insert`) and Huntress does GW API insertion. Verdict: **injection is table stakes; how you inject is the differentiator** (§3).
- *KnowBe4 "Defend for Google Workspace"* is dated 2026-09-15 in the switching brief — that is 11 days in the future. Treat as a date error (likely 2025-09-15) or a pre-announcement; either way the incumbent is moving into Gmail-native detection at the enterprise end.

## 4. Positioning lines we can stand behind (honest, underpromise)

1. **"Phishing drills for Google Workspace, delivered through the Gmail API. No allowlists, no spam-filter surgery."** — True by construction; hits gap #1 and #3; matches the language admins used when they left for Phin.
2. **"Indicators, not verdicts. A scanner click is not a fail. A report is always a win."** — Hits the 3-year-unfixed FP gripe and the employee resentment; commits us to visible confidence labels, which we intend to ship anyway.
3. **"We measure how fast your people report, not how many we tricked."** — Google's fire-drill framing in our words; report rate and time-to-first-report as the default dashboard. Defensible against UCSD/ETH Zurich, which undermine click-rate as a metric.
4. **"No seats. No minimums. No renewal call."** — The only claim in this list that needs no engineering; durable even if a paid module is ever a named decision later. (Avoid "free forever" — it pre-empts a decision the north-star reserves.)
5. **"Your key stays in your tenant."** — Self-hosted answers the DMI "persistent connection to a US console" and "vendor staff can impersonate admins" objections, and the Google-blog "allowlists get left in place" point. This is the security-angle line; pair it with the injection audit log.

**Lines to refuse:** any efficacy number ("cut phish-prone from 40% to 4%" — the RCTs say the category can't promise that); "AI-powered" (2026 "AI slop" backlash); "KnowBe4 killer" or anything about the founder; "replaces your security awareness program" (we don't have the library); "MSP-ready" until MTP integration exists.

## 5. North-star flags

- **Underpromise (Work-style #1):** the honest claim on simulations is *drill + reporting speed*, not behaviour change; the durable value is the detection track (report button + triage + rip). Lead the roadmap and the copy with Track A.
- **Security angle (Product #1):** gap #9 (key-in-tenant, least-privilege scopes, injection audit log, banner emulation not bypass) is the compliance story — make it a shipped, testable feature, not copy.
- **Free vs paid (OBILABS-ALIGNMENT):** the phishing module is free/community today; every line above is worded so a later paid module or hosted offer is a named decision, not a broken promise. No hosting claims beyond "coming soon."
- **Build window:** everything in §2 marked "bones" (event stream with real+sim, scanner-confidence field, report/time-to-report fields, training seam, aliases in the identity model) must be in the frozen schema now; the positioning depends on those fields existing, not on the dashboards.
- **Community presence:** Reddit punishes astroturfing; any post must be plainly labelled as the maintainer, and led with the customer problem — not the founder story.
